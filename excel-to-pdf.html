How to convert Excel to PDF — private & free

  1. Choose a spreadsheet (.xlsx, .xls, .csv, or .ods).
  2. Click "Convert & download".
  3. Each sheet becomes a landscape table with the header row repeated on every page.

Frequently asked questions

Is my spreadsheet uploaded when I convert it?

No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser — the file never leaves your device.

Does it keep formulas and formatting?

Cell values are printed as displayed (formula results, formatted numbers and dates). Colours, merged cells, charts, and images are not reproduced — the output is a clean, printable table.

What if my sheet is very wide?

Columns are scaled to fit the page width; very wide sheets get narrow columns with wrapped text. For best results, split wide sheets or hide unneeded columns first.
