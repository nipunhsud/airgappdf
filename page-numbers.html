How to add page numbers to a PDF — private & free

  1. Choose a PDF file.
  2. Click "Number & download".
  3. Each page gets a "page / total" stamp at the bottom center.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded when adding page numbers?

No. The numbers are stamped entirely in your browser — the file never leaves your device.

Where do the page numbers appear?

Bottom center of every page, in the format "page / total" (e.g. 3 / 12).

What if my PDF already has page numbers?

The stamp is drawn on top of the existing content, so you may end up with two sets — remove or crop existing numbers first if that matters.
