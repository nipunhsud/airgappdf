How to rotate a PDF — private & free

  1. Choose a PDF file.
  2. Pick a rotation: 90° clockwise, 180°, or 90° counter-clockwise.
  3. Click "Rotate & download".

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded when I rotate it?

No. Rotation happens entirely in your browser and the file never leaves your device.

Does rotating change quality?

No. Rotation is lossless — it only updates each page’s rotation setting, so text stays selectable.

Can I rotate a single page?

This tool rotates every page at once. To rotate one page, split it out first, rotate it, then merge it back.
