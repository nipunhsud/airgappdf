How to convert text or Markdown to PDF — private & free

  1. Choose a .txt or .md file, or paste text into the box.
  2. Pick "Plain text" to keep line breaks as-is, or "Markdown" to render headings, lists, and code blocks.
  3. Click "Convert & download".

Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded?

No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser — the file never leaves your device.

What Markdown is supported?

Headings (#), paragraphs, bulleted and numbered lists, horizontal rules, fenced code blocks, and inline bold/italic/links (rendered as plain text). Tables and images are not rendered.

Can I convert a Word document?

Use the Word to PDF tool for .docx files. This tool is for plain text and Markdown.
