How to split a PDF — private & free

  1. Choose a PDF file.
  2. Enter the pages to keep, e.g. 1-3,5.
  3. Click "Extract & download" to get a new PDF with just those pages.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded when I split it?

No. Page extraction runs entirely in your browser — the file never leaves your device.

Can I split a PDF into several files?

The tool extracts one range per run into a new PDF. Run it again with a different range to produce more files.

Does splitting reduce quality?

No. Pages are copied losslessly into the new PDF — text stays selectable and images are untouched.
