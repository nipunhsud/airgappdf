How to delete pages from a PDF — private & free

  1. Choose a PDF file.
  2. Enter the pages to delete, e.g. 2,5-7.
  3. Click "Delete & download" to get a new PDF without those pages.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded when I delete pages?

No. Pages are removed entirely in your browser — the file never leaves your device.

Does my original file get modified?

No. You download a new PDF; the original on your device is untouched.

Does deleting pages reduce quality?

No. Remaining pages are copied losslessly — text stays selectable.
