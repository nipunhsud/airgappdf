Frequently asked questions

Is my Word document uploaded when I convert it to PDF?

No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser — the file never leaves your device.

Will the PDF look exactly like Word?

Not pixel-perfect. The converter keeps headings, paragraphs, lists, and inline images, laid out in a clean standard font. Custom fonts, columns, text boxes, and complex tables are simplified. For an exact replica, use Word's own "Save as PDF".

Which formats are supported?

DOCX (Word 2007 and later). Older .doc files must be re-saved as .docx first. For plain text or Markdown, use the Text to PDF tool.
